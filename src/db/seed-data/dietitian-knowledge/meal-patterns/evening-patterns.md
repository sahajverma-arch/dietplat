# Evening Meal Patterns

## Tea plus one light item

The evening slot is a tea break, not a meal — a cup of chai (or a herbal/spiced variant) alongside one light snack item: roasted makhana, a handful of nuts, a small savory snack, or occasionally just a piece of fruit or a light cereal item. It should never carry the size or dish count of lunch or dinner.

## Never a heavy or fried main dish

A substantial cooked dish, a sabzi, or a heavy fried item does not belong at evening — that kind of item reads as a second dinner, not a tea-time snack, and crowds out the appetite for the actual dinner that follows a couple of hours later.

## One snack item, not a spread

Similar to mid-morning, evening should carry a single snack item rather than several different small dishes plated together — real tea-time snacking is one thing eaten alongside the tea, not a mixed platter.
