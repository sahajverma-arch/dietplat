# Maintenance Meal Construction Principles

## Balance over restriction

A maintenance target doesn't call for the portion discipline of a fat-loss plan or the deliberate calorie-density of a muscle-gain plan — meals can follow the region's ordinary, everyday pattern without needing to be trimmed down or bulked up in either direction.

## Variety matters more here than tight repetition

Since there's no aggressive calorie or macro pressure driving specific choices, a maintenance week has more room to genuinely rotate through different dishes across the week rather than repeating the same handful of "safe" items — this is the goal where a real, varied regional identity can show through most clearly.

## Sustainable, not aggressive, is the standard

The test for a maintenance week isn't "is this the most optimized possible plate" — it's "could a client eat like this indefinitely without it feeling like a diet." Familiar, everyday combinations that match how the client's region actually eats are more appropriate here than anything that reads as a temporary regimen.
