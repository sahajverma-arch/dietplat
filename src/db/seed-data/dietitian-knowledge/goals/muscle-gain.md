# Muscle-Gain Meal Construction Principles

## Protein spread across most meals, not backloaded

Rather than concentrating the day's whole protein target into one large dinner, a muscle-gain day distributes protein sources across breakfast, lunch, and dinner (and often a snack too) — dal, paneer, curd, eggs, or lean meat showing up repeatedly through the day rather than only once.

## Carbohydrates support the higher calorie target

A muscle-gain day needs a genuinely generous cereal/rice portion, not the moderated portion appropriate to a fat-loss day — a bigger roti or rice serving at both lunch and dinner is expected and appropriate here.

## Calorie-dense additions are welcome, not avoided

Nuts, ghee, full-fat dairy, and paneer are useful tools for hitting a higher calorie target without needing an unrealistically large volume of food — a muscle-gain plan shouldn't shy away from these the way a fat-loss plan moderates them.

## A substantial breakfast, not a light one

Where a fat-loss breakfast might stay simple and light, a muscle-gain breakfast can reasonably be heavier — a stuffed paratha plus a genuine protein source (egg, paneer, or a thicker curd) rather than the lighter default.
