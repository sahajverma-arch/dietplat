# Fat-Loss Meal Construction Principles

## Protein anchors every main meal

On a fat-loss target, each of the day's substantial meals should carry a clear protein source — a dal, paneer, egg, or lean meat/fish dish, not just a cereal-and-vegetable plate. Protein is what keeps a calorie-reduced day feeling satisfying rather than just smaller.

## Vegetable volume does real work

Leaning on generous vegetable portions — a real sabzi serving, not a token garnish — adds volume and fiber to a meal without adding much in the way of calories, which helps a lower-calorie day still feel like a full plate.

## Moderate the cereal portion, don't eliminate it

A fat-loss target calls for a more moderate roti/rice portion than a maintenance or muscle-gain day, but the cereal base should still be present in some real amount at lunch and dinner — removing it entirely in favor of an all-protein, all-vegetable plate is not how a realistic fat-loss diet is actually built.

## Avoid stacking two rich or fried items in one meal

On a calorie-constrained day in particular, a meal shouldn't combine two independently rich elements — a fried snack alongside a ghee-heavy main dish, for instance. One indulgent element per meal, at most, keeps the day's fat and calorie totals realistic without feeling deprived.
