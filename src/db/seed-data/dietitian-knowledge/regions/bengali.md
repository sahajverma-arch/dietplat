# Bengali Regional Diet Patterns

## Rice as the daily staple, not wheat

Like the South, Bengali meals are built around rice rather than roti — a Bengali week should be rice-forward, with wheat-based breads appearing only occasionally rather than as the default cereal.

## Mustard oil and panch phoron define the flavor base

Mustard oil, and the five-spice panch phoron tempering, give Bengali cooking a flavor identity distinct from the ghee-forward north or the coconut-forward south — this shows up across both vegetarian and fish preparations.

## Fish is a genuine everyday staple, not an occasional dish

For non-vegetarian Bengali clients, fish is a routine, everyday protein rather than a special-occasion item the way it might be treated elsewhere — a Bengali non-vegetarian week should reflect that with real frequency, not token appearances.

## A distinct sweet course tradition

Bengal has a particularly strong dessert and sweets tradition (often milk- or curd-based), which is worth acknowledging as a genuine regional identity — though within a structured weekly plan, this shows up more as an occasional, deliberate inclusion than a daily one.
