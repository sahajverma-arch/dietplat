## Meal Structure
- Breakfast: Warm herbal tea; Fresh fruits with low-fat yogurt, avoid mangoes/bananas/chikoos; Whole grain toast (1)
- Mid-Morning: Buttermilk
- Lunch: Soup or vegetable juice; Sprouts and salad with tofu; Whole-wheat toast (1)
- Evening: Coconut water; Porridge, oatmeal/broken wheat (1 bowl)
- Dinner: Rotis (2 small); Vegetables (1 bowl); Salad with sprouts or chicken

## Reasoning
"Nourishing your body through the day prevents extremes of hunger that lead to over-eating, binging and fatigue — work toward a pattern of six meals a day, portions modified based on your body's signals. Low fat does not mean unlimited."
