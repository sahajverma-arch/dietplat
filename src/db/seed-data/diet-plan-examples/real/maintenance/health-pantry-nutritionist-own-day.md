## Meal Structure
- Morning: Warm water (3-4 glasses); Herbal tea, ginger cumin mulethi lemongrass
- Post-Workout: Smoothie with vegan protein powder, soaked sesame seeds, seasonal fruit, dates, almonds, hemp hearts, coconut oil
- Lunch (12:00-12:30 PM): Rice or ragi roti; Dal; Vegetable; Beetroot or carrot with green chutney or lime pickle
- Afternoon: Black coffee or black tea (1 cup)
- Evening (4:00-4:30 PM): Bhel with vegetables, roasted chana and pumpkin seeds OR poha OR dhokla OR uttapam with coconut chutney
- Dinner (7:00-7:30 PM): Vegetables and bean/dal/tofu OR cheelas OR oven-roasted vegetables and tofu OR missi roti with sabzi
- Post-Dinner: Chamomile tea or rose tea or hot water

## Reasoning
Food choices tied directly to the author's own stated goals — exercise capacity, hair/immune health, sleep quality, and craving control — rather than restriction or calorie-counting.
