## Meal Structure
- Early Morning (6:00-7:00 AM): Lukewarm water (1 glass); Soaked almonds (4-5) or soaked raisins
- Breakfast (8:00-9:00 AM): Vegetable poha OR vegetable upma OR oats porridge OR idlis with sambar (2); Milk OR curd OR boiled egg
- Mid-Morning Snack (11:00 AM): Seasonal fruit, apple/guava/orange/papaya/banana (1); Unsalted nuts or seeds (1 small handful)
- Lunch (1:00-2:00 PM): Whole wheat rotis (2) OR brown rice; Dal OR rajma/chole (1 bowl); Seasonal vegetable preparation; Curd (1 bowl); Salad
- Evening Snack (4:00-5:00 PM): Roasted chana, sprouts chaat, fruit chaat, buttermilk, or green tea with roasted nuts/seeds
- Dinner (7:30-8:30 PM): Rotis (1-2) OR small brown rice serving; Dal OR grilled paneer/fish/lean chicken; Cooked vegetables; Fresh salad
- Bedtime: Warm milk with turmeric (1 small glass)

## Reasoning
Built around commonly available Indian foods, designed for practical implementation without requiring expensive ingredients — explicitly non-restrictive, sustainable framing, not a weight-loss plan.
