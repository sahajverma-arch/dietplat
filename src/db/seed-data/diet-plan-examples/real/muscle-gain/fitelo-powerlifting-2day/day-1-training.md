## Meal Structure
- Morning-Breakfast: Large red potato (1); Chicken breast (170g)
- Pre-Workout: Protein pancakes with sugar-free syrup; Oats (0.5 cup); Whole eggs (2)
- During Workout: Intra-workout supplement with carbs
- Post-Workout: Protein shake with sugary cereal, immediately after; Sweet potatoes (2 large); Oats (1.5 cups); Chicken breast with BBQ sauce (170g)
- Lunch: Rice (0.5 cup); Steak (170g)
- Dinner: Steamed vegetables; Chicken breast (170g)

## Reasoning
Powerlifters need to consume more calories than they burn to build muscle and strength — roughly 1.6-2.2g protein/kg, 3-6g carbs/kg, and fat at 20-30% of total calories.
