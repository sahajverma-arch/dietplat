## Meal Structure
- Morning-Breakfast: Oats (0.5 cup); Egg whites (8); Whole eggs (2)
- Mid-Morning Snack: Roasted almonds and walnuts (1 handful); Unsweetened green tea (1 cup)
- Lunch: Grilled tofu or paneer (150g); Brown rice (1 cup); Sauteed spinach
- Evening Snack: Boiled egg (1); Roasted chickpeas (1 handful)
- Dinner: Grilled salmon (150g) OR lentils for vegetarians (1 cup); Roasted vegetables, zucchini carrots mushrooms (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Powerlifters need to consume more calories than they burn to build muscle and strength — roughly 1.6-2.2g protein/kg, 3-6g carbs/kg, and fat at 20-30% of total calories.
