## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Lukewarm water with lemon (1 cup); Tea, no sugar (1 cup); Marie biscuits (2)
- Breakfast: Ragi ganji/Rava ganji (1 glass); Idli with sambar OR Neer dosa with coconut chutney (2)
- Mid-Morning: Fruit salad (1 medium bowl); Green tea, no sugar (1 cup)
- Lunch: Ragi ball/steamed rice (1 bowl); Koshimbiri, vegetable salad (1 bowl); Bus saaru (1 bowl); Cabbage palya (1 bowl)
- Evening: Green tea, no sugar (1 cup); Wheat rusk (2)
- Dinner: Chapati, no oil (2); Bhendekai gojju (1 small bowl); Curd (1 medium bowl)

## Reasoning
South Indian cuisine's richness is controlled through portion size and cooking method while keeping real native dishes (koshimbiri, ragi ganji, bhendekai gojju) rather than replacing them.
