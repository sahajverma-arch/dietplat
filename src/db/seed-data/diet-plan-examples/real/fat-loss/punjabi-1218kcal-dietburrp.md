## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Lukewarm water with lemon (1 cup); Tea, no sugar (1 cup); Marie biscuits (2)
- Breakfast: Methi & onion paratha, no fat (2 medium); Curd (1 bowl)
- Mid-Morning: Apple or grapefruit (1 medium)
- Lunch: Green split mung dal khichdi with vegetables (1 medium bowl); Onion raita (1 small bowl); Mixed vegetable salad (1 bowl)
- Evening: Tea, no sugar (1 cup); Wheat rusk (2)
- Dinner: Missi roti, no oil (2); Mixed vegetables (1 medium bowl); Curd (1 small bowl)

## Reasoning
Traditional Punjabi diets were built for manual-labor lifestyles matched to ghee-heavy food; modern sedentary Punjabis need technique substitution within the same cuisine (tandoor instead of ghee-fry) rather than abandoning it.
