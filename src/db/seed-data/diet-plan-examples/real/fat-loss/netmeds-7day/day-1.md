## Meal Structure
- Breakfast: Vegetable poha, minimal oil (1 bowl); Green tea (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Apple/guava/seasonal fruit (200g)
- Lunch: Dal/sambar (1 cup); Brown rice/quinoa (1 cup); Mixed vegetable sabzi (1 cup); Salad (1 cup)
- Evening: Roasted chickpeas or fox nuts (1 handful)
- Dinner: Whole wheat roti (1); Palak paneer, minimal oil (1 cup); Yogurt (1 bowl)

## Reasoning
The amount of calorie intake and spending determines weight management — the plan advocates portion control combined with exercise for sustainable results.
