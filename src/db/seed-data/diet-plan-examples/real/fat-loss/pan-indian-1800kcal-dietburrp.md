## Meal Structure
- Breakfast: Sugar-free muesli with low-fat milk (1 bowl) OR 2 egg whites with whole wheat bread (2 slices) and milk (1 cup) OR chapatis with milk (2) and walnut halves/almonds (2-4)
- Mid-Morning: 1 fruit, excluding banana/chikoo/sitaphal/mango/grapes
- Lunch: Salad (1 bowl); Multigrain flour chapati (2); Brown rice/daliya (0.5 cup); Vegetable (1 cup); Dal (1 cup); Curd/yogurt (1 cup) OR chicken (2 pieces) OR fish (1 piece)
- Evening Snack: Coffee (1 cup); Sukha bhel (1 cup) OR roasted poha chivda (0.5 cup) OR roasted chana (0.5 cup)
- Dinner: Vegetable soup/chicken broth (1 bowl); Vegetable pulao (1 bowl) OR bajra roti (2 small) with vegetable gravy (1 cup) and curd/vegetable raita (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Milk (1 cup)

## Reasoning
The plan avoids starvation via 3 meals and 3 snacks scattered through the day, framing diet as a lifestyle change rather than restriction — meals balanced for age, BMI, gender, and activity level, satiety driven by fruits/vegetables, low-fat dairy, lean meat, and legumes.
