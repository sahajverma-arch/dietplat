## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Plain lukewarm water (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Dhadpe Pohe (1 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Soaked nuts (1 fistful)
- Lunch: Green salad (1 cup); Multigrain chapati (1); Methi (1 cup); Varan/Tur dal (1 cup); Dahi (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Green tea (1 cup)
- Evening Snack: Roasted poha chivda (1 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Tawa pulao (1 cup); Raita (1 cup); Tomato saar (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Sol kadhi (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
