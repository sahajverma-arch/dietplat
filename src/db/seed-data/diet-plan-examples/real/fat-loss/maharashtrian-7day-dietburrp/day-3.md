## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Chia water, soaked overnight (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Mung usal (1 cup); Wheat chapati (1); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Watermelon (100-150g)
- Lunch: Green salad (1 cup); Bhakri/Jowar (1); Zunka (1 cup); Vangi Bharta (1 cup); Dahi (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Green tea (1 cup)
- Evening Snack: Thepla (1); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Multigrain chapati (1); Fanas bhaji (1 cup); Chawli usal (0.5 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Ginger tea (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
