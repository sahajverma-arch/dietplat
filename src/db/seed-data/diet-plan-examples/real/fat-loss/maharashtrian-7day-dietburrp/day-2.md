## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Jeera water (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Vegetable Upit, rawa + vegetables (0.5 cup + 0.5 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Orange (1)
- Lunch: Green salad (1 cup); Multigrain chapati (1); Ladies finger vegetable (1 cup); Vatana Usal (1 cup); Dahi (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Taak/Curd (1 cup)
- Evening Snack: Roasted poha chivda (1 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Varan Bhat, varan + brown rice (1 cup + 0.5 cup); Amaranth vegetable (1 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Cinnamon tea (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
