## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Jeera water (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Phodni chapati (1) with vegetables (0.5 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Boiled egg whites (2)
- Lunch: Palak soup (1 cup); Bajra bhakri (1); Bhopla vegetable (1 cup); Kala Chana Amti (1 cup); Curd (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Muskmelon (100-150g)
- Evening Snack: Aalu vadi, shallow fried/steamed (4-6 pieces); Green chutney (1 tbsp); Coffee without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Masala bhat, red rice + vegetables (1 cup); Bhajleli vangi kaap (3-4 pieces); Buttermilk (1 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Warm water with lemon (1 glass)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
