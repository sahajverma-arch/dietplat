## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Lemon water (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Egg bhurji, 1 egg with onion/coriander; Multigrain bread (2 slices); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Green tea (1 cup)
- Lunch: Khawang Kakdi (1 cup); Wheat chapati (1); Farasbi bhaji (1 cup); Chana dal fry (1 cup); Taak (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Green tea (1 cup)
- Evening Snack: Sweet potato and chicken cutlet (2 small); Coffee without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Dhirde/Jowar (1 medium); Fish curry, non-fried (1 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Sol kadhi (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
