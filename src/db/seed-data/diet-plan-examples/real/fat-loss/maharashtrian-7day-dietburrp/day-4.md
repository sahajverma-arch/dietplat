## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Barley water, soaked overnight (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Rajgira Chilla (2 small); Green chutney (1 tbsp); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Soaked nuts — almonds, walnuts, dates, raisins (1 fistful)
- Lunch: Green salad (1 cup); Nachni bhakri (1); Turai Bhaji (1 cup); Maharashtrian Kadhi (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Taak/Curd (1 cup)
- Evening Snack: Kothimbir vadi, shallow fried (6 pieces); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Dinner: Multigrain chapati (1); Fanas bhaji (1 cup); Chawli usal (0.5 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Nutmeg tea (1 cup)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
