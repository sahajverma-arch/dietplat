## Meal Structure
- Early Morning: Lemon water (1 glass)
- Breakfast: Vegetable Poha (0.5 cup); Tea without sugar (1 cup)
- Mid-Morning: Egg whites (2) OR seasonal fruit
- Lunch: Kakdi koshimbir (1 cup); Bhakri (1); Shepu vegetable (1 cup); Varan (1 cup); Taak (1 cup)
- Mid-Afternoon: Apple (1)
- Evening Snack: Churmure bhel (1 cup)
- Dinner: Thalipeeth (1) OR Chicken Biryani, brown rice + 100g chicken (1 cup); Curd (1 cup); Green salad (1 cup)
- Bedtime: Warm lemon water (1 glass)

## Reasoning
Modifications stay within authentic Maharashtrian food culture — white rice swapped for brown/red rice, coconut/groundnut-heavy preparations for pulses, reduced oil/ghee — rather than abandoning the cuisine.
