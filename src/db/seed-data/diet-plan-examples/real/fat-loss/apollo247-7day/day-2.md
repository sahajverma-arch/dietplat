## Meal Structure
- Breakfast: Oats with milk and nuts (1 bowl)
- Mid-Morning: 1 fruit, apple or pear
- Lunch: Roti (1); Dal; Palak paneer, low-fat paneer
- Evening: Buttermilk; Makhana (1 handful)
- Dinner: Grilled fish/chicken with stir-fried veggies

## Reasoning
Indian cuisine is rich in whole grains, lentils, vegetables, and spices — the plan leans on high fibre, protein-rich foods, healthy fats, and low glycemic index foods to prevent blood sugar spikes.
