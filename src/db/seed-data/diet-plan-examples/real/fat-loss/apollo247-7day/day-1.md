## Meal Structure
- Breakfast: Moong dal chilla (2) with mint chutney
- Mid-Morning: Sprouts (1 small bowl)
- Lunch: Roti (1); Brown rice (0.5 cup); Dal; Sabzi, lauki or bhindi
- Evening: Green tea; Roasted chana (1 handful)
- Dinner: Vegetable khichdi (1 bowl); Curd

## Reasoning
Indian cuisine is rich in whole grains, lentils, vegetables, and spices — the plan leans on high fibre, protein-rich foods, healthy fats, and low glycemic index foods to prevent blood sugar spikes.
