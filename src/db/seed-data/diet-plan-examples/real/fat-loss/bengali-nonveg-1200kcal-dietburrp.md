## Meal Structure
- Breakfast (8-9 AM): Rice OR Besan cheela with onions (2) OR Chirer Pulao (1 soup bowl); Tea/coffee with 50ml milk (150ml); Apple (1)
- Mid-Morning (11-12 noon): Buttermilk/ghol (150ml) OR Sprouts salad (100g)
- Lunch (1-2 PM): Wheat bran chapati, no oil (2); Chicken curry OR rui/katla machher jhol OR Arhar dal (50g); Saag-vegetable dishes (100g); Salad (100g)
- Evening (4-5 PM): Murmura chaat/Jhaal Muri (100g) OR Steamed corn (100g); Tea/coffee with 50ml milk (150ml)
- Dinner (8-9 PM): Poha with onions and tomato (100g) OR Wheat bran chapati (1); Green veg salad (100g)

## Reasoning
Real Bengali dishes (machher jhol, poha, jhaal muri) are kept via portion control rather than elimination — "all the good things come in small packets" — a response to rising obesity/diabetes in the region.
